# Acme Co. Knowledge Base (sample)

## Refund policy
Customers may request a full refund within 30 days of purchase. Refunds are
processed to the original payment method within 5–7 business days. Items marked
"final sale" are not eligible for a refund.

## Shipping
Standard shipping takes 3–5 business days. Express shipping (1–2 business days)
is available at checkout for an additional fee. We currently ship to the US,
Canada, and the EU.

## Support hours
Support is available Monday to Friday, 9am–6pm Eastern Time. The fastest way to
reach us is the in-app chat. Email support typically responds within one
business day.
